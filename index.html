  1. By tube Baker Street or Regent's Park

    Both stations are about a 10 minute walk. Baker Street is served by the Bakerloo, Jubilee, Metropolitan, Circle and Hammersmith & City lines.

  2. By bus Marylebone Road

    Several routes stop along Marylebone Road. Hop off near York Gate and walk north into the park.

  3. In the park Enter at York Gate

    Walk up towards York Bridge. Just before the bridge, take the path on your left along the Boating Lake. We are the brick pavilion with the green awning.

  4. Bikes and parking Racks outside the door

    Bike racks sit beside the terrace. Pay and display parking is available on the Outer Circle.
